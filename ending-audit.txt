CROW MAULER ENDING AUDIT — v1.7
Audited against the installed game database and v1.5 NPC rules, 2026-10-10.
Spoilers below. These are mod adaptations, not claims of canonical Mauler endings.

DECISION
Keep the solo exploration routes E, B and D with Mauler-specific, text-only
conclusions and a credits card that returns to the title. Preserve guards ignoring
Mauler and everyone else's hostile/fleeing behavior. Do not restore recruitment
or dialogue to force the human endings to work. Original-character games retain
the original scripts and endings.

ALL MAIN ENDINGS

E / escape:
  Potential progression block: a living Le'garde flees instead of running the
  event that enables switch 1000 (YOU_CAN_ESCAPE). The dead-prisoner interaction
  can still enable it. Human/party exit conversations and the mercenary S check
  also remain in the original exit lists.
  Fix: reaching map 24 (level-7 prison) enables escape. Fortress exit events on
  maps 74 and 131 retain that prerequisite and lead directly to the Mauler E
  epilogue. Hard-mode human S flags do not select Cahara's ending.
  Play: reach the level-7 prison, then return to the fortress entrance and leave.

B / traces of Gro-goroth:
  This is the natural solo route. The native post-victory choices are silenced,
  but its B flag/transfer is outside those branches, so silence alone does NOT
  make this ending impossible. However, it retains human dialogue/choreography
  and becomes unsafe with old Girl scene flags or the generic NPC replacement.
  Fix: the five Gauntlet threshold events (map 142 events 25,27,29,30,31) select
  the solo controller (event 26). It runs original troop 169, with no escape;
  victory alone reaches the B epilogue. Defeat retains the game's game-over
  common event. The stray optional god battle at event 47, whose win sets the
  generic S flag, is disabled in Mauler games.
  Play: cut the three organs, descend through the God of the Depths, traverse
  the Gauntlet, and defeat the Traces of Gro-goroth.

C / defeat Le'garde, and C / submit to Le'garde:
  Both require bringing Le'garde to the throne. Mauler cannot recruit him.
  In a partially progressed save, his event flees, and the invisible controller's
  two dialogue choice sequences are cancelled. This can leave a locked scene.
  Fix: neither is a supported Mauler route. The shared final Void triggers on
  maps 140,160,185 resolve to Mauler's D ascension instead. An old save already
  in the final human ending map gets an explicitly labelled recovery epilogue.

D / ascend as a new god:
  The Endless Soul is gated by accepting Nilvan's request in map 138 event 3;
  silence cancels the choice, losing the key while completing the dream.
  The throne controller (map 146 event 4) also waits for a human stand-in to
  move. The NPC hostility handler can erase that stand-in. The final troop 122
  is chiefly a narrative/choice scene, ending with Abort Battle, not an ordinary
  boss fight. Suppressing its choices can prevent switch 1888 and hang it.
  Fix: Nilvan withdraws and leaves the Endless Soul when the player reaches her
  dream through the Tower's existing route. The throne keeps its interaction,
  transfers directly into the Void without waiting for the human stand-in,
  and the final Void trigger starts the original two-headed Crow Mauler
  encounter (troop 135), using stronger enemy records 529-532. It has a
  7,000-HP torso, two 900-HP heads and a 900-HP maul arm. Original battle
  scripts and attacks remain intact. Escape is disabled; only victory awards D. Existing Void traversal and any original
  difficulty-specific encounters before that trigger remain in place.
  Play: obtain the Cube of the Depths, explore Ma'habre, complete the soul/key
  route, defeat Francois, claim the throne, then defeat the two-headed Mauler
  at the end of the Void. The D epilogue acknowledges defeating the other Mauler.

A / God of Fear and Hunger:
  Requires the Girl in the party; her transformation has several sprite/autorun
  stages (map 142 event 24; map 159). No recruitment makes it unreachable, and
  forcing those flags can leave missing actors/stalled transformation scenes.
  Fix: the normal final chamber consistently selects solo B. Old saves in map
  159 return to map 142's solo finale. No Girl is recruited or manufactured.

ALL FOUR HUMAN S ENDINGS

Cahara:
  His treasure chain can set switch 2662 (map 111 event 270) because Mauler uses
  actor slot 1. The old fortress exit could therefore award a human S ending
  and show Cahara's life story for Mauler. Mauler exits now always select E.

D'arce:
  Requires her resurrection route and Le'garde's transformation (map 24 event
  169 page 19, switch 2656 -> 2249). Mauler is not D'arce, cannot recruit her,
  and the living Le'garde event flees. Not a supported Mauler route.

Enki:
  Requires the Enki initial-character branch and equipped Spirit Anchor (armor
  53) at the final Void scene. Mauler cannot equip armor and occupies actor 1.
  Not a supported Mauler route; Void completion consistently selects D.

Ragnvaldr:
  Common event 248 requires actor 5, eight boss-soul armors including Crow
  Mauler's armor 36, and the four greater soul items. Actor 5 cannot join and
  the original Crow Mauler fight is deliberately suppressed. Not a supported
  Mauler route; no false boss kill or free Crow Mauler soul is awarded.

ENDING PRESENTATION AND RECOVERY
Map 86 event 4 has separate pages for E,D,C,C2,B,A,generic S and each human S.
Mauler uses a dedicated text scene instead of those human cinematics. It has
three pages (two epilogue pages plus credits), Enter/click navigation, and a
working return to title. It does not claim the original human-ending Steam
achievements. Existing B/E terminal saves get the corresponding mod epilogue. Old D saves
without a recorded Void victory, plus A/C/S terminal saves, get an explicitly
labelled recovered completion. New D completions require the boss victory.
The v1.7 migration clears serialized obsolete ending interpreters only on the
specified cinematic maps. Normal dungeon saves keep their event progress.

PROGRESSION GATES
- Cube of the Depths: the pedestal interactions (map 22 events 83,85-89) are
  object events and remain usable without trading or dialogue. No free cube.
- Three organs: native object interactions on maps 24/127/141 set 1750/1751/1752;
  map 17 opens the mouth when all three are cut. No automatic organ completion.
- Endless Soul: granted once at Nilvan's dream; repaired if an older Mauler save
  already set dream-complete flag 1805 but lost the soul to cancelled dialogue.
- Enlightened/Tormented/Domination souls: real corresponding boss victories
  preserve their progression rewards, including when the NPC adapter bypasses
  a separate reward controller. Native reward commands cannot duplicate them.
  A saved local Francois defeat also restores his missing throne/reward flags.
- No change to Mauler stats, attacks, sprite scale, guard recognition or original
  Crow Mauler suppression outside the specifically tagged Void boss events.

SCOPE AND VERIFICATION
Dungeon Nights has its own ending map 161 and naming-based setup; it is not a
supported Mauler campaign or part of these main-game ending adaptations.
Audited all ending flags in map, troop and common-event data. Automated runtime
checks cover the Nilvan key/return, escape gate, Gro-goroth win, throne transfer,
Void boss stats, suppression isolation, defeat not awarding D, victory and
epilogue rendering/title return, and legacy ending handling.
Battle victory tests remove enemy HP to test transitions; they are not balance
proofs. This is not a complete start-to-finish manual campaign playthrough.
Automated development tests are not included in the player download.
