PLAYABLE CROW MAULER — GAMEPLAY REFERENCE

COMBAT VALUES

The standard single-headed enemy's torso (Enemies.json #127) supplies:
  HP 5000; MP 0; ATK 10; DEF 10; MAT 10; MDF 10; AGI 10; LUK 10.
  Physical hit 95%; evasion 5%; normal physical attack element.
  Blindness (#49) and enemy confusion (#28) immunity; preserve TP.
  Enemy Auto Battle is removed so you can control the character.
  Equipment is disabled: the maul and body are innate, preserving these values.
  Other applied status effects can still change the effective statistics.

Original body-part HP (reference, NOT extra player health bars):
  Torso 5000; head 500; left arm 700; right arm 700; each leg 200.
  The head has 85% physical evasion and 50% magic evasion, and is vulnerable
  to blindness. Those are HEAD TARGET statistics, not torso-wide bonuses.
  Harder enemy records include a 7000-HP torso and 900-HP head/maul arm.
  This mod consistently uses the STANDARD 5000-HP form on every difficulty.
  The double-headed mauler2 is a separate encounter and is not this character.

ATTACKS

Attack / Maul (clone of skill #93):
  40 HP damage, 10% variance, 95% success, certain-hit type, no critical.
  Fracture (#19) at 100%; arm infection (#56) at 30%; leg infection (#55)
  independently at 30%. Original attack animation #68. No MP or TP cost.
  Fracture applies its original traits, including max HP multiplied by 0.8.
  Infection flags are applied exactly; enemies do not have the player's
  out-of-battle infection countdown (common event #192).

Peck (clone of skill #85):
  Original physical hit/evasion rules, 100% skill success before hit/evasion,
  Headless (#31) at 100% on a successful eligible hit, animation #69,
  no MP/TP cost, +5 TP. The source's formula "30" is inactive because its
  damage type is NONE; this is a decapitation effect, not 30 damage.
  Original common event #240 mutilates player actors. The clone omits that
  event and instead kills the selected enemy battler/part after Headless
  successfully applies. Enemy immunities are respected. A multipart boss's
  normal troop events decide what losing that part does to the entire boss.

Flock of crows (clone of ENEMY skill #161):
  20 HP damage, 20% variance, certain hit, 40% permanent blindness (#49),
  animation #124, single target, one hit, no MP/TP cost, no critical.
  This is not player spell #149 (70 damage x3 for 30 MP).

Empty stare (clone of skill #94):
  Original do-nothing action. No damage, no effects, no cost.

All four actions appear in Skills. Attack also performs Maul.
Run remains available. Talk is removed in Mauler runs, including older saves.
NPCs do not offer dialogue, trading or recruitment to Crow Mauler.

NPC REACTIONS (MAULER RUNS ONLY)

* Living dungeon guards, elite guards, ballista guards and Moonless guards
  ignore the player. Their GUARD_OUTFIT event-page condition is treated as
  satisfied; guard chase and contact battles are suppressed. This does not
  equip armor, add armor statistics, change the player's sprite, or enable
  the disguise switch globally for other creatures.
* Living NPCs with an existing battle start it on contact/interaction or within
  three tiles with a clear path of sight. Explicit character identities prevent
  a multi-character cutscene from selecting another character's battle.
* NPCs without a battle retreat when approached (six tiles) or interacted with.
  They move away and fade out, including trapped NPCs who cannot find a path.
  Their departures and local battle defeats persist in Mauler saves.
* Friendly dialogue, shops, recruitment and player Talk skills are unavailable.
  Named dialogue from invisible cutscene controllers is also suppressed.
  Object descriptions, ordinary doors and corpse/loot pages remain available.
* NPC battle victories retain their direct native defeat/loot flags. Narrative
  branches and conversations are bypassed. Mauler has three supported solo
  endings with repaired progression gates; see ENDINGS below.
* Original-character runs retain the original NPC behavior.

ENDINGS

* E — Beyond the Gates: reach Le'garde's level-7 prison, then leave through
  the fortress entrance. His dialogue/recruitment is not required.
* B — The Last Inspection: cut all three organs, descend through the God of
  the Depths, cross the Gauntlet and defeat the Traces of Gro-goroth.
* D — The Eternal Warden: complete the Ma'habre soul route, defeat Francois,
  claim the throne, cross the Void and defeat the two-headed Crow Mauler.
  This finale uses the original stronger mauler2 records: 7,000-HP torso,
  two 900-HP heads and a 900-HP maul arm, with the original troop attacks.
  No escape; victory is required for Ending D. Nilvan withdraws from
  her dream and leaves the Endless Soul without requiring conversation.
* All three have Mauler epilogues, credits and a return to title. The Girl's A,
  both Le'garde C endings and the four human S endings are not Mauler routes.
  Older saves already inside incompatible finale scenes have recovery handling.
* Completed Nilvan dreams and saved Francois defeats repair missing quest gates.
  No free Cube of the Depths or organ completion. The Void boss must be fought.
* These are mod endings and do not award the original human-ending achievements.
  Detailed findings for every ending are in ending-audit.txt.
* Save, fully close and restart the game to load v1.7, then load your Mauler save.

ADAPTATIONS AND LIMITS

* One controllable actor uses the torso's health and traits. Six independently
  targetable player body parts are NOT implemented. Original body-part records remain in your installed game database.
* Two freely chosen actions per turn represent the boss's maul arm plus head.
  The original enemy head randomly chooses Peck, stare, or Flock at turn end;
  a player can choose instead. Its forced target selection does not run against your own party.
* Actor 1 is the campaign compatibility slot in Mauler runs. This preserves
  hunger, inventory and actor-specific event wiring. Recruitment is blocked.
  Mauler-specific text endings replace the human ending cinematics.
  A custom text intro replaces the human backstory. Both intro choices use the game's original skip-intro setup for
  world generation and difficulty. The story is also in intro-text.json.
* MP stays 0. Scripted human sanity checks receive a stable sanity value;
  human phobia states are excluded. This prevents zero monster MP from
  immediately producing human insanity penalties. Hunger remains active.
* Uses the existing mauler1 walking sheet (index 0). The player battle sprite
  is the supplied crow_mauler_player.png in img/sv_actors/, displayed at 350.2px
  height (70% larger than v1.2) with its proportions and transparency preserved. Only a player actor
  flagged as Crow Mauler uses it. Enemy art and menu portraits stay original.
  Battle art is static with engine movement/effects, not an animated sheet. Injury
  rules still run, but bespoke missing-limb walking/battle artwork is absent.
* Original Crow Mauler NPCs, roaming controllers, arrival announcements and
  battles (including sleep ambushes and the double-headed variant) are disabled
  only in Mauler runs, except the deliberately enabled two-headed Void finale.
  Beds use their existing safe-rest branch and remain
  usable. This does not award boss loot or set the boss-defeated switch. Loading
  an existing Mauler save refreshes the map to remove spawned Mauler NPCs.
* Original enemy skills and database JSON files remain unchanged. New skills
  occupy IDs 341–344; another mod using those IDs will require reconciliation.
* Mod saves require this plugin. Original saves have no Mauler flag and retain
  their original actors. Existing save files were not edited during testing.
* Dungeon Nights is not a supported Mauler mode. The ending transitions have
  automated runtime coverage; a full manual campaign playthrough is still untested.
