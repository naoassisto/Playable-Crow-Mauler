# Playable Crow Mauler 1.7.0-beta.1

Playable Crow Mauler for Fear & Hunger 1, by Naoassisto. Requires your own compatible copy of the game; the game is not included.

## Choose a download

- **Playable-Crow-Mauler-1.7.0-beta.1-Manual.zip** — recommended. Five mod files with manual installation and removal instructions.
- **Playable-Crow-Mauler-1.7.0-beta.1.zip** — the same gameplay files, plus optional Windows/macOS backup and uninstall helpers.
- **.sha256** files — checksums for the corresponding ZIPs.

Extract the ZIP before installing. Back up your saves and the game's `js/main.js`. Follow the included README; merge folders instead of replacing them. On the title screen, choose **Crow Mauler**, then **Enter the dungeon**.

## Included

- 5,000 HP, two actions per turn, Maul, Peck, Flock of crows, and Empty stare.
- Custom rear-view battle sprite and optional text intro.
- Guards ignore Mauler; other identified living NPCs fight or flee.
- Adapted solo endings E, B, and D, including a two-headed Void finale.
- Local database compatibility check and licensed attack-animation fix.

## Beta limitations

Tested English database: `System.versionId 14145329`. Translations, other builds, and database-altering mods may be rejected by the offline compatibility check. Mods replacing `js/main.js` require a merge; skill IDs 341–344 are reserved by this mod. Dungeon Nights is unsupported.

Automated loader, packaging, and Bash helper checks pass. Earlier gameplay checks covered starts, intro, attacks, saves, NPCs, and ending transitions. Native Windows gameplay and PowerShell helper execution remain unverified, as do a complete manual campaign and a full final-boss balance pass.

See the [installation guide](https://github.com/naoassisto/Playable-Crow-Mauler#install), [source files](https://github.com/naoassisto/Playable-Crow-Mauler#files), and [issue tracker](https://github.com/naoassisto/Playable-Crow-Mauler/issues).
